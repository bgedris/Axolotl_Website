What is an Axolotl?

The axolotl (Ambystoma mexicanum) is a unique salamander native to Mexico. Unlike most amphibians, axolotls retain their larval features into adulthood—a phenomenon known as neoteny.

Cool Facts

  • Super Regeneration: They can regrow lost limbs, tails, organs, and even parts of their brain!
  • Color Varieties: Wild axolotls are dark brown or black, but captive ones come in pink, white, and golden shades.
  • Critically Endangered: Wild axolotls are severely endangered due to habitat pollution and invasive species.

Basic Aquarium Care

  • Cool Water: Keep tank temperature between 60°F and 68°F (16°C – 20°C).
  • Substrate: Use fine aquarium sand or a bare-bottom tank to avoid accidental ingestion of gravel.
  • Low Flow: Use gentle filtration so strong currents don't cause stress.
